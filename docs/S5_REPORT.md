### Figure 1 — the two placements (Section 2, inline TikZ)

A two-panel schematic of iteration (1). Panel **(B)** puts the smoother in the `q̂_{t+1}` slot,
inside the loop the saturating integrator closes; panel **(A)** puts it downstream of a completed

agreeing with the body, which it does.

### Figure 2 — the coverage cliff (Section 3, `figures/figure1_boundary.pdf`)

Generated by `src/make_figure1.py`, which lists every `(file, field path)` it reads in its header.

| source | what is read |
|---|---|

| `results/forfeit-20260820T063132Z-83747c45.json` | `aggregate_table[*].{arm, params.tau, regime, T, miscoverage}` — panel (b), the eleven-point grid |

Panel (a) plots realised miscoverage against dead-band width τ under all five admissible

**The figure is honest about its own limits, and this was tightened twice.** Only the null

caption and Limitations now all say so.

## 4. The 0.100035-at-`T = 10⁶` question — this session's highest-priority item

**Resolved by printing the right number, not by hedging.**

The abstract read: *"partial adjustment there overruns Proposition 2's bound 42× at T = 10⁶ and
still covers, **0.100035**."* Table 1 had a `miscov. 2×10⁵` column and no `10⁶` miscoverage column
at all.

The measurement **exists at the right horizon**. In
`results/forfeit-20260820T063045Z-83747c45.json`, `aggregate_table`, arm `ema_w0.999`, regime
`adversarial`:

| horizon | miscoverage | ratio |
|---|---|---|
| `T = 2×10⁵` | **0.100035** | 47.2282 |
| `T = 10⁶` | **0.100004** | 42.0978 |

So `42×` was a `10⁶` figure and `0.100035` was the `2×10⁵` figure, in one clause implying both were
at `10⁶`. **The fix printed the measured `10⁶` value, `0.100004`**, and **Table 1 gained a
`miscov. 10⁶` column** so the number is visible in the table it points at. All eleven values were
re-derived twice more — once by the orchestrator, once by the adversarial critic — and match.

**A footnote was never on the table, and the honest coda is that the number is no longer in the
abstract at all.** Wave 3's rewrite dropped the `42×` / `10⁶` / `0.100004` group **as a unit**,
deliberately, precisely so the miscoverage figure could not be re-paired with a wrong horizon a
