\begin{abstract}
A printed claim that the scorecaster breaks online conformal coverage meets its
refutation by overlapping authors; this paper joins them and proves where one legal
perturbation family fails. Conformal PID states long-run coverage for any bounded
scorecaster; a corollary keeps it for predictable perturbations of the deployed
threshold inside an admissible radius. The claim was acted on; placement and derivation
are conceded by name. At the null scorecaster the condition is \emph{tight}: a legal
$L_1$ dead band on the \emph{completed} threshold leaves that set at that radius itself.
Past it miscoverage is $1.000000$ at $\alpha = 0.1$: coverage goes, not just the
rate. The edge is the saturator--scorecaster pair's, not the readout's; elsewhere the
condition is sufficient only. Under the equally legal $\hat{q} \equiv +b/2$ the failing
band covers; at $\hat{q} \equiv -b/2$ both it and partial adjustment at $w = 0.999$
miscover every round.
\end{abstract}
