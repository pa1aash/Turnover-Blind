\begin{abstract}
Conformal PID states long-run coverage for an arbitrary bounded scorecaster, and
a published corollary retains its rate under any predictable perturbation of the
deployed threshold inside an admissible radius. The opposite reading is in print
too, under overlapping authorship, and acted on. We make two claims. The record
holds both halves and we join them, conceding placement and derivation by name.
And at the null scorecaster the corollary's condition is \emph{tight}: a legal
$L_1$ dead band on the \emph{completed} threshold leaves that set past
$\tau^{\star} = \sup_x r_t(x) + \sup_t \hat{q}_t - b/2$, which is the published
radius itself there, and past it realised miscoverage is $1.000000$ against
$\alpha = 0.1$ while saturation fires every round. Leaving the set costs coverage
and not only the rate: partial adjustment there overruns Proposition~2's bound
$42\times$ at $T = 10^{6}$ and still covers, $0.100004$. The edge belongs to the
saturator--scorecaster pair, not to the readout, and elsewhere the condition is
sufficient only: under the equally legal $\hat{q} \equiv +b/2$ the failing band
covers, and under $\hat{q} \equiv -b/2$ both the failing band and partial
adjustment at $w = 0.999$ return $1.000000$.
\end{abstract}
