\begin{abstract}
Conformal PID states long-run coverage for an arbitrary bounded scorecaster, and
a published corollary retains its rate under any predictable perturbation of the
deployed threshold inside an admissible radius. The opposite reading is in print
too, under overlapping authorship, and acted on. We make two claims. The record
holds both halves and we join them, conceding placement and derivation by name.
And the corollary's condition is tight: a legal $L_1$ dead band on the
\emph{completed} threshold leaves that set at $\tau^{\star} = \sup r_t +
\sup\hat{q}_t - b/2$, its own radius at the null scorecaster, past which realised
miscoverage is $1.000000$ against $\alpha = 0.1$ while saturation fires every
round. Leaving it costs coverage, not only the rate: partial adjustment there
overruns Proposition~2's bound $42\times$ at $T = 10^{6}$ and covers, $0.100035$
at $2{\times}10^{5}$. That edge belongs to the saturator--scorecaster pair: under
$\hat{q} \equiv +b/2$, equally legal, the failing band covers; under $\hat{q}
\equiv -b/2$ both readouts return $1.000000$.
\end{abstract}
