\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

An online conformal method emits a threshold before each observation and moves it afterwards.
Where the interval feeds a decision carrying an incumbent state that movement is charged, and
forecast stability names it, scores it and prices it
\citep{tunc2013nervousness,godahewa2025stability,vanbelle2023deepstability,%
pritularga2024congruence,genov2026switching,vanbelle2026stabilizing}. Holding a forecast's level
fixed and comparing what its temporal structure does was invented earlier, in forecast
verification, where the predictive marginal is matched on real producers and only the dependence
varies \citep{gneiting2007calibration,pinson2012scenarios,worsnop2018scenarios}; that coverage and
mean length under-describe a deployed interval is established too
\citep{min2026questioning,vaze2026simultaneous}. None of it is claimed here.

\paragraph{The record contradicts itself, and the halves sit in different papers.} Conformal PID
\citep{angelopoulos2023pid} adds a \emph{scorecaster} $\hat{q}_{t+1}$ to a saturating error
integrator and asks of it only that it be predictable and lie in $[-b/2,b/2]$ --- ``any function of
the past'' (Theorem~1) --- so a movement penalty placed in that slot is already quantified over. Also in print, of the same
method: ``[s]ince using a scorecaster (D-part) in C-PID breaks the theoretical coverage guarantee,
we only use the quantile tracker and error integrator'' \citep{li2024neuralconformal} --- one
sentence in the baselines paragraph of that paper's experiments, stated and acted on there, and
refereed: it survives verbatim on printed page $18443$. The general statement is in print too,
twice and with proof:
\citet[Corollary~2]{li2025o2cp} add an arbitrary predictable $d_t$
with $|d_t| \le \mu_t(b/2-|\hat{q}_t|)$ to the deployed threshold, observe that the result ``has
exactly the CPID error-integration form'', and conclude $|E_T| \le c\,h(T)+1 = o(T)$; the same
generic-slot argument carries AcMCP's long-run coverage \citep{wang2026acmcp}. The first and last authors of \citet{li2025o2cp} are the authors
of \citet{li2024neuralconformal} and cite it, so the halves already share a bibliography; this is
ordinary self-correction. What is useful is that four surfaces --- arXiv
full text and abstracts, OpenReview, and the six works Semantic Scholar records as citing it ---
return zero works printing both. A refereed 2026 paper separately calls the choice of
scorecaster model ``arbitrary'' and lacking ``principled guidance''
\citep{hu2026distinformed} while asserting valid coverage itself: a complaint about model
selection and variance, not about validity. \textbf{We claim neither the placement nor the
derivation.}

\paragraph{What that leaves open is the outside of the admissible set.} Corollary~2 is a
\emph{retention} result: perturbations within $\mu_t(b/2-|\hat{q}_t|)$ keep $|E_T| \le c\,h(T)+1$.
The modification a stability-motivated designer reaches for leaves that set --- a smoother on the
\emph{completed} output. Section~\ref{sec:forfeit} measures what that costs and where it becomes
a coverage failure.
