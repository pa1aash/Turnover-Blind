\section{Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Two producers, not one.} At time $t$ a forecaster fit on data before $t$ induces a
score $s_t$; a threshold $q_t$ is emitted before $y_t$ is revealed; the deployed set is $C_t
= \{ y : s_t(x_t,y) \le q_t \}$ and $\mathrm{err}_t = \mathbf{1}\{ s_t(x_t,y_t) > q_t \}$; the
target is long-run coverage, $\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t \le T}\mathrm{err}_t = \alpha + o(1)$, which
carries no probabilistic model on the data. The two producers used here are different
machines. ACI \citep{gibbs2021aci} manipulates the miscoverage level, $\alpha_{t+1} =
\alpha_t + \gamma(\alpha - \mathrm{err}_t)$, and deploys $q_t = \hat{Q}_t(1-\alpha_t)$.
Conformal PID \citep{angelopoulos2023pid} manipulates the threshold on the score scale,
\begin{equation}
  q_{t+1} \;=\; \hat{q}_{t+1} \;+\; r_t\Big( \textstyle\sum_{i \le t} (\mathrm{err}_i - \alpha) \Big),
  \label{eq:pid}
\end{equation}
their iteration~(5), in which $\hat{q}_{t+1}$ is a \emph{scorecaster}, ``any function of the
past: $x_i, y_i, q_i$, for $i \le t$'', and $r_t$ saturates: their condition~(4) requires
$r_t(x) \ge b$ for $x \ge c\,h(t)$ and $r_t(x) \le -b$ for $x \le -c\,h(t)$, with $h$
\emph{admissible} --- nonnegative, nondecreasing, sublinear. The ACI recursion manipulates
$\alpha_t$ alone and offers a penalised value nothing to be added to. Throughout
$\mathrm{err}_t$ is computed on the deployed $q_t$, and boundedness is read on the realised
scores, which is what the proof of Proposition~2 uses.

\paragraph{The movement penalty, in both forms.} With $\hat{q}^{\mathrm{raw}}_{t+1}$ any
scorecaster clipped to the observed score range, we carry two one-scalar penalties and this
draft chooses between them nowhere:
\begin{equation}
  \underbrace{\hat{q}_{t+1} = (1-w)\,\hat{q}^{\mathrm{raw}}_{t+1} + w\,\hat{q}_t}_{L_2,\; w \in [0,1)}
  \qquad
  \underbrace{\hat{q}_{t+1} = \hat{q}_t + S_\tau\big(\hat{q}^{\mathrm{raw}}_{t+1} - \hat{q}_t\big)}_{L_1,\; S_\tau(u) = \mathrm{sign}(u)(|u|-\tau)_+ ,\; \tau \ge 0}
  \label{eq:penalty}
\end{equation}
% [OPERATOR INPUT] Q7 -- L1 versus L2 is UNANSWERED and is deliberately left open
% here. Both forms are presented; the experimental section must either sweep both
% or record the operator's decision. Do not silently drop one.
Both are convex combinations $\hat{q}_{t+1} = (1-\lambda_t)\hat{q}_t + \lambda_t
\hat{q}^{\mathrm{raw}}_{t+1}$ with $\lambda_t \in [0,1]$: $\lambda_t = 1-w$ for $L_2$, and
$\lambda_t = (1 - \tau/|\hat{q}^{\mathrm{raw}}_{t+1} - \hat{q}_t|)_+$ for $L_1$, via $S_\tau(u)
= u(1-\tau/|u|)_+$. Two details are load-bearing: the anchor is the previous \emph{slot} value
$\hat{q}_t$, not the deployed $q_t$, since anchoring to $q_t$ returns the unsaturated
integrator output to the slot and puts a second accumulator in series with the first; and the
shrinkage takes a unit step, an over-relaxed $\kappa>1$ leaving the interval spanned by its
two arguments once $|\Delta_t| > \tau\kappa/(\kappa-1)$.

\paragraph{Placements, and what is measured.} The penalty admits at least three
architecturally distinct positions in (\ref{eq:pid}): \textbf{(A)} downstream of the
completed $q_{t+1}$; \textbf{(B)} in the $\hat{q}_{t+1}$ slot; \textbf{(C)} inside the
integrator's argument, in place of $\mathrm{err}_i$. We compare A and B; C is named because
\citet{dupuy2026relevance} and \citet{wu2025eci} occupy it and both report that the quantity
the integrator drives to $\alpha$ is then no longer the miscoverage sum. Per arm we report
realised coverage, mean interval width $\mathbb{E}[L]$, the deployed threshold's
\emph{travel} $\mathcal{T}_H = \sum_{t=2}^{H} |q_t - q_{t-1}|$ split into the two shares of
$\Delta q_t = \Delta\hat{q}_t + \Delta r_{t-1}$, and the turnover and net log growth of a
position charged to move. Coverage and $\mathbb{E}[L]$ are matched across arms;
$\mathcal{T}_H$ and the decision cost are what vary. $\mathcal{T}_H$ is half-width turnover
only for a symmetric score with a frozen base forecaster: a refit forecaster also moves the
centre, and the CQR score carries two thresholds.

\paragraph{What is inherited, and what is not.} Under Placement~B both penalties in
(\ref{eq:penalty}) keep $\{\hat{q}_t\}$ inside the convex hull of $\hat{q}_1$ and the raw
scorecaster's range, so it is a bounded past-measurable sequence in $[-b/2,b/2]$, while
(\ref{eq:pid}) and $r_t$ are untouched. Theorem~1 of \citet{angelopoulos2023pid} therefore
applies unmodified and its Proposition~2 supplies $|\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t \le T}(\mathrm{err}_t
- \alpha)| \le (c\,h(T)+1)/T$ with the constants of the unpenalised method, a convex
combination neither enlarging $\sup|\hat{q}|$ nor forcing a larger $b$. \textbf{No new theorem
is required and none is claimed}; Section~\ref{sec:related} records who states this already.
What is not inherited is any statement about movement: Theorem~1 transfers coverage and only
coverage, and the integrator's share of $\Delta q_t$ is the part the penalty does not reach.
That share obeys an exchange relation: writing $\varepsilon$ for Proposition~2's bound at
horizon $T$ and $M$ for the integrator's expected per-step contribution to travel,
$\varepsilon M = 2\alpha(1-\alpha)b/T$ exactly for a constant-gain integrator --- the gain
cancels and the penalty weight is absent --- and the product is likewise constant in $T$ for
the tangent integrator.
