Of the $862$ references returned for Table~\ref{tab:bridge}'s twenty-six anchors, $13$ fall in a
neighbouring literature and twelve of those sit in three forecast-stability papers:
\citet{vanbelle2026stabilizing}, co-authored by Pierre Pinson, cite eight works of verification,
\citet{genov2026switching} three of smoothed online optimisation, and \citet{zanotti2026smqc}
build their intervals out of conformal prediction. The edge exists where an
author bridges it, and the cross-citation is concentrated rather than sparse. \textbf{We claim no
disconnection}, and the counts above do not support one: the arXiv full-text index that returns
zero for the switching-cost method names paired with ``conformal'' over-matches phrases, so its
zeros are conservative and its positives are not counts, and an exact instrument refutes the
reading they invite --- of the $875$ works citing \citet{kalai2005lazy}, two are online conformal,
\citet{chen2023ipoc}, cited here, and \citet{zhang2024bayesianocp}. Four fields name one quantity
many ways; the object that settles its validity sits in one.

\paragraph{What is conceded, by name.} The placement is prior work:
\citet{angelopoulos2023pid} state coverage for any admissible integrator and any scorecaster and
run a smoothing-family model in that slot themselves; \citet{dupuy2026relevance} give the generic
argument in their Appendix~A and \citet{duerst2024calibrating} adopt that
scorecaster. The derivation is prior work too, and that is the
concession that matters most: \citeauthor{li2025o2cp}'s Corollary~2 proves the retention statement
in Conformal PID's own notation \citep{li2025o2cp}, with \citet{wang2026acmcp} an independent
second instance. The smoother object, the metric arithmetic and both readout maps are conceded in
Section~\ref{sec:setup}, and \citet{zheng2025scdsplit,wu2025eci,chen2023ipoc,lade2026bcaci} each
place a smoother inside their own validity argument. One vocabulary out, this is integrator
anti-windup with a feedforward path, where free-until-the-bound-binds is the defining
specification rather than a finding \citep{galeani2009antiwindup}. Nearest in the
other direction, \citet{gao2025coloke} run the same recursion but gate the \emph{model} update on
the conformal score; their one non-standard assumption is that $\sum_{t \le T} q_t \le
O(\alpha\,h(T))$ for a sublinear $h$, supported with a plot and left to later work to derive. That accumulation is what Section~\ref{sec:forfeit} measures diverging.
